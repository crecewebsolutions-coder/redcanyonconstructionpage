Welcome to the official legal terms and warranty documentation of Red Canyon Remodeling LLC (“Contractor”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). By engaging our services, signing an architectural construction agreement, receiving estimates, or commissioning residential remodeling work, you (“Client”, “Homeowner”, or “Property Owner”) agree to the following contractual terms, warranties, and operating standards.

Policies & Service Conditions

The Core Operational Charter of Red Canyon Remodeling LLC
1. Estimates & Project Pricing: Estimates are provided based on the initial project scope and details shared by the client. Final pricing is confirmed in a written agreement before work begins. Any requested modifications or unforeseen conditions will be discussed and approved prior to proceeding.
2. Payments & Agreements: Projects require a signed agreement and an initial deposit before scheduling or starting work. Remaining payments are structured in clear milestones outlined in the contract, ensuring transparency and alignment throughout the project.
3. Changes & Additional Work: Any additions, material upgrades, or design changes requested after work begins will be documented through a written change order outlining the updated cost and timeline before execution.
4. Construction Timelines: Estimated schedules are established before construction starts. While we make every effort to maintain projected completion dates, timelines may occasionally adjust due to permit approvals, material lead times, or unexpected site discoveries.
5. Permits & Building Standards: All construction, electrical, and plumbing work is conducted in accordance with applicable local building codes. When permits are required, we coordinate the application and inspection process to ensure full regulatory compliance.
6. Quality & Workmanship: We are committed to delivering high-quality craftsmanship, durable materials, and professional execution. Every project undergoes thorough quality reviews before final completion to guarantee client satisfaction.
7. Customer Communication: Clear, timely communication is fundamental to our process. Clients receive regular updates regarding progress, next steps, and any key decisions needed along the way.
8. Privacy & Customer Information: All personal details, project documentation, and property information shared with Red Canyon Remodeling LLC are kept strictly confidential and used solely for the purpose of planning and executing your project.
Our Commitment: “At Red Canyon, our goal is not simply to build structures, but to build lasting relationships through quality, integrity, and dependable service.”

1. Scope of Work & Fixed-Price Architectural Proposals

All residential remodeling projects performed by Red Canyon Remodeling LLC are governed by an itemized, written Scope of Work (SOW). Our proposals detail:

  • Specific structural demolitions, framing modifications, and wall removals.
  • Detailed specifications of finishes, including slab quartz/granite, cabinetry construction, porcelain tile layout, and premium hardware.
  • Dedicated mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) upgrades certified to local building codes.
  • Estimated milestone durations and substantial completion target dates.

Any modifications, additions, or reductions to the initial Scope of Work must be documented in a written and countersigned Change Order prior to physical execution on site.

2. Our Industry-Leading Craftsmanship Warranty

At Red Canyon Remodeling LLC, we build with uncompromising precision and premium materials. Unlike conventional contractor warranties that expire after only one year, we proudly back all our work with a comprehensive, written Comprehensive Craftsmanship Warranty and permanent structural commitment covering all labor and installation integrity, beginning from the formal Substantial Completion Date.

What Is Covered:

  • Structural Framing & Load-Bearing Modifications: Integrity of headers, load redistribution, wall relocations, and framing alignments.
  • Tile & Stonework Installation: Adhesion, waterproofing membranes (including Schluter-KERDI / hydro-barrier shower pans), grout stability, and non-settlement cracking.
  • Cabinetry & Millwork Installation: Fastening, leveling, hinge alignment, crown molding joins, and architectural millwork stability.
  • Plumbing & Electrical Fixture Installation: Rough-in connections, junction boxes, drain slopes, shower valves, and water supply fittings installed by our licensed trade team.
  • Drywall, Floating & Paint Application: Seam taping, Level 5 smooth finishes, texture adhesion, and trim caulking under normal structural conditions.

What Is Excluded:

  • Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratching of natural wood/stone caused by everyday usage, or harsh abrasive chemical cleaning.
  • Manufacturer defects in owner-supplied appliances, lighting fixtures, or third-party mechanical equipment (these remain covered under their respective manufacturer warranties).
  • Damage resulting from foundational shifting, seismic activity, exterior flooding, extreme thermal stress, or homeowner neglect.
  • Modifications, repairs, or tampering by unauthorized third-party trades or subcontractors not contracted directly through Red Canyon Remodeling LLC.

3. Milestone Payment Schedules & Lien Protection

We operate strictly under transparent milestone schedules tied directly to verifiable physical progress. We do not demand inflated upfront retainers. Our standard residential remodeling payment protocol follows:

  • Initial Commitment Deposit (10% to 15%): Secures project scheduling, engineering approvals, architectural drawings, and permit applications.
  • Demolition & Rough-In Milestone: Released following completed demolition, framing, and passing city rough mechanical/plumbing/electrical inspections.
  • Finishes & Cabinetry Milestone: Released following drywall completion, custom cabinet installation, and countertop template verification.
  • Substantial Completion Milestone: Released upon completion of tile setting, plumbing trim, electrical fixtures, and primary paint finish.
  • Final Punch List Retainage (5% to 10%): Held by the homeowner until the walkthrough punch list is 100% completed and approved.

With every completed milestone disbursement, Red Canyon Remodeling LLC provides executed Conditional and Unconditional Lien Waivers, legally protecting your home against any subcontractor or supplier mechanics' liens.

4. Permitting, Code Compliance & HOA Coordination

Red Canyon Remodeling LLC is a licensed and registered residential contractor. We maintain full liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage for all project personnel.

  • Municipal Building Permits: When structural modifications, main electrical panel upgrades, or plumbing reroutes require municipal permits, we prepare, submit, and manage all city inspections.
  • HOA Architectural Review: We provide full architectural elevations, material spec sheets, and work hour schedules necessary for your Homeowners Association (HOA) Design Review Committee submissions.
  • Historical & Local Ordinances: All work conforms to the International Residential Code (IRC) and local municipal amendments.

5. Change Order Policy (Zero Surprises)

Unforeseen substrate conditions (such as hidden historical water damage, rotted subfloors, or unpermitted legacy electrical wiring discovered behind drywall during demolition) are immediately photographed and brought to the homeowner's attention.

No work that alters the project cost will ever proceed without a signed, written Change Order. The Change Order will clearly detail:

  • The nature and photographic proof of the uncovered issue.
  • The required engineering or corrective labor and material cost.
  • The exact impact (if any) on the projected project completion timeline.

6. Clean Jobsite Discipline & Property Protection

We recognize that your home is your sanctuary. Our daily jobsite rules are non-negotiable:

  • Dust Barrier Enclosures: Heavy-duty ZipWall magnetic zipper containment partitions and negative-pressure HEPA air scrubbing units are deployed on all indoor remodels.
  • Surface Armor: Ram Board and Neoprene runners protect all non-remodeled hardwood, stone, and carpet pathways from entry door to workspace.
  • Daily Debris Cleanup: Sites are swept, vacuumed with HEPA filters, and cleared of hazardous debris at the conclusion of every workday.
  • Strict Noise & Hours Policy: High-impact work is strictly confined to standard residential work hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).

7. Homeowner Responsibilities

To ensure smooth project flow and timely delivery, the homeowner agrees to:

  • Provide safe, unobstructed access to the work area, water, and electrical supply throughout agreed work hours.
  • Finalize client-selected material selections (e.g., tile models, plumbing finishes, paint colors) by the designated cutoff schedule to prevent supply-chain delays.
  • Keep domestic pets and children safely separated from active construction zones.

8. Dispute Resolution & Mutual Respect

In the rare event of a dispute, both parties commit to resolve matters professionally through good-faith executive discussion. If a dispute cannot be resolved directly, the parties agree to binding mediation under the American Arbitration Association construction rules prior to initiating legal court proceedings.
