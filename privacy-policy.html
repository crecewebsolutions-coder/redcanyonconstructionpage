At Red Canyon Remodeling LLC (“we”, “us”, or “our”), we hold client privacy and homeowner security to the highest professional standards. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, safeguard, and use personal information gathered through our website, online estimate forms, direct email communications, and on-site remodeling consultations.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you intentionally provide to us when requesting an estimate, submitting architectural inquiries, or entering into a remodeling contract:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, email address (redcanyonred@gmail.com is our recipient), telephone details if voluntarily provided in correspondence, and preferred communication method.
  • Project Property Information: Physical property address or neighborhood location, existing square footage, approximate home age, and structural layout details.
  • Project Specifications: Desired remodeling spaces (kitchen, spa bathroom, closet, whole-home, garage), budget targets, timeline expectations, architectural inspiration photos, blueprints, and design notes.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Standard anonymous server log statistics (browser type, device screen size, referring page) collected solely to ensure proper website responsiveness and speed across mobile and desktop devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is utilized exclusively for genuine remodeling and customer service operations:

  • To prepare accurate, transparent, and itemized architectural estimates.
  • To coordinate on-site structural consultations and initial walkthroughs.
  • To procure necessary municipal building permits, HOA architectural approvals, and engineering stamps required for construction.
  • To maintain continuous communication regarding project milestones, material delivery schedules, and change order authorizations.
  • To fulfill our Craftsmanship Warranty obligations and respond to post-completion inquiries.

3. Zero Sale or Commercialization of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, monetize, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or lead generation services.

Your contact information and project details remain strictly confidential within Red Canyon Remodeling LLC and the certified trade personnel directly executing your residential project.

4. Jobsite & Project Photography Confidentiality

As an architectural design-build contractor, photographic documentation is an essential part of quality control, milestone verification, and our portfolio showcase. However, we maintain strict privacy protocols regarding jobsite media:

  • Address Anonymity: We never publish house numbers, street signage, vehicle license plates, or geo-locatable landmarks in public portfolio photos.
  • Personal Belongings: We take meticulous care to exclude family photographs, personal documents, valuables, safes, or security alarm panels from public imagery.
  • Explicit Consent: Homeowners retain the full right to request that interior photographs of their residence remain private and excluded from our public portfolio or social media channels.

5. Information Sharing with Essential Partners

We share project information only when strictly necessary to fulfill your remodeling contract:

  • Municipal Building Departments: Submitting architectural floor plans and property addresses to obtain official building, plumbing, and electrical permits.
  • Licensed Structural Engineers & Specialists: Providing structural measurements to certified engineers for beam calculation and load-bearing review.
  • Specialty Material Suppliers: Providing delivery addresses and contact names for direct jobsite drop-offs of cabinetry, quartz slabs, or custom doors.

All partners and suppliers are bound to respect the confidentiality of your property and may not use your information for external marketing.

6. Data Security & Storage

We employ physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All digital correspondence and estimate requests are transmitted through secure protocols and stored within password-protected, encrypted business environments.

7. Homeowner Rights & Record Retention

You have the right to:

  • Request a summary of the personal contact and project data we have on file for your residence.
  • Request updates or corrections to any out-of-date contact details.
  • Request deletion of your non-contractual records from our active marketing contacts. (Note: Legally required construction contracts, invoices, and permit records must be preserved for tax, licensing, and warranty limitation periods).

8. Updates to this Privacy Policy

Red Canyon Remodeling LLC may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect evolving industry standards, state licensing mandates, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us Regarding Your Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your property data is managed, please contact our administrative team:
