Policies & Service Conditions

1. Estimates & Project Pricing: Estimates are provided based on the initial project scope and details shared by the client. Final pricing is confirmed in a written agreement before work begins. Any requested modifications or unforeseen conditions will be discussed and approved prior to proceeding.
2. Payments & Agreements: Projects require a signed agreement and an initial deposit before scheduling or starting work. Remaining payments are structured in clear milestones outlined in the contract, ensuring transparency and alignment throughout the project.
3. Changes & Additional Work: Any additions, material upgrades, or design changes requested after work begins will be documented through a written change order outlining the updated cost and timeline before execution.
4. Construction Timelines: Estimated schedules are established before construction starts. While we make every effort to maintain projected completion dates, timelines may occasionally adjust due to permit approvals, material lead times, or unexpected site discoveries.
5. Permits & Building Standards: All construction, electrical, and plumbing work is conducted in accordance with applicable local building codes. When permits are required, we coordinate the application and inspection process to ensure full regulatory compliance.
6. Quality & Workmanship: We are committed to delivering high-quality craftsmanship, durable materials, and professional execution. Every project undergoes thorough quality reviews before final completion to guarantee client satisfaction.
7. Customer Communication: Clear, timely communication is fundamental to our process. Clients receive regular updates regarding progress, next steps, and any key decisions needed along the way.
8. Privacy & Customer Information: All personal details, project documentation, and property information shared with Red Canyon Remodeling LLC are kept strictly confidential and used solely for the purpose of planning and executing your project.
Our Commitment: “At Red Canyon, our goal is not simply to build structures, but to build lasting relationships through quality, integrity, and dependable service.”
