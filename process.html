Structured Methodology

The 5-Phase Construction Roadmap & FAQ

How we eliminate project anxiety through meticulous scheduling, transparent milestone billing, clean jobsite discipline, and white-glove delivery.

01
Phase 1: Discovery

Site Consultation & Feasibility

We visit your property to evaluate existing structural walls, plumbing stacks, electrical loads, and spatial proportions, aligning your lifestyle goals and target budget.

Phase Deliverables:
  • ✓ Architectural laser dimension audit
  • ✓ Structural loadbearing inspection
  • ✓ Preliminary budget & timeline roadmap
02
Phase 2: Planning

Design Drafting & Fixed Contract

Our team drafts exact cabinet elevations, lighting schemes, and stone cut sheets. We produce an unambiguous, line-by-line contract with fixed material allowances.

Phase Deliverables:
  • ✓ Cabinet millwork & electrical schematics
  • ✓ Exact material selection catalogue
  • ✓ 100% Fixed-price proposal & schedule
03
Phase 3: Site Prep

Dust Containment & Clean Demo

Before swinging a hammer, we isolate the remodel area. Heavy Ram Board protects your floors, ZipWall barriers prevent dust escape, and surgical demolition begins.

Phase Deliverables:
  • ✓ Certified Ram Board floor masking
  • ✓ Negative-pressure HEPA air filtration
  • ✓ Controlled daily debris haul-away
04
Phase 4: Craftsmanship

Master Framing, Stone & Finishing

Laser-straight framing, rough-in mechanicals, Schluter shower waterproofing, custom cabinet installations, waterfall marble fabrication, and Level 5 smooth drywall.

Phase Deliverables:
  • ✓ Weekly photographic milestone reports
  • ✓ Precision stone & millwork setting
  • ✓ Continuous lead contractor oversight
05
Phase 5: Delivery

White-Glove Inspection & Warranty

We conduct a comprehensive punch-list walkthrough with you. The home receives a post-construction detail clean, and your official Craftsmanship Warranty certificate is issued.

Phase Deliverables:
  • ✓ 100% completed client punch-list
  • ✓ Post-construction detail clean
  • ✓ Official Written Craftsmanship Warranty
